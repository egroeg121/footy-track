    Temporal candidates through the trajectory solver: does 5-channel input help once a solver ranks?¶

    Branch exp/temporal-detector. Code: experiments/temporal_detector/{clip_infer,pipeline_eval}.py, Prefect flow temporal-clip-infer/temporal-clip-infer (~/code/jobs/footy_temporal_clips.py). Harness: exp/offline-ball-tracking @ fc05732 (src/footy_track/ball_offline/, imported read-only; its numbers are reproduced exactly below). Follows temporal_detector_ablation.md (v1: YOLO11s, 100 epochs, 2 seeds, arm A 3ch vs arm B 5ch).

    Question and answer¶

    Does the 5-channel detector's candidate set help once a trajectory solver (the harness's B1: offline Viterbi + short gap fill, camera on) does the ranking?

    Yes, modestly, and the gain survives the solver; but the YOLO11s detectors stay below the production RT-DETR-L unless their confidence is recalibrated, and then only match it on the test clips.

    • 9 harness test clips (170 clean GT balls: 110 seg010, 53 seg031, 7 single frames): B1(5ch) - B1(3ch) recall@10 = +0.047 [+0.012, +0.086] with the harness calibration knots applied to the YOLO confidence ("raw"), +0.029 [+0.003, +0.059] with per-model isotonic maps fitted on v11 train ("iso"); per seed +0.059 / +0.035 (raw), +0.041 / +0.018 (iso). The candidate ceiling is almost unchanged (+0.009 [-0.020, +0.038]): on these clips the solver gets more out of the 5ch candidates, it does not get more candidates. Precision is unchanged with raw knots (-0.003 [-0.018, +0.010]) and lower with iso (-0.046 [-0.078, -0.018]): the iso map is fitted on train images the detector has memorised, so it trusts almost every candidate (equal-precision min_conf falls to 0.10) and lets the solver emit more wrong points.
    • arsenal_example (250 frames, a clip with no train frames, 176 labelled frames): B1(5ch) - B1(3ch) recall@10 = +0.071 [+0.034, +0.111] (raw; A is nearly blind: 0.139) and +0.253 [+0.202, +0.304] (iso; A 0.196 -> B 0.449), ceiling +0.216 [+0.168, +0.267] (0.213 -> 0.429). Per seed +0.062 / +0.080 (raw), +0.261 / +0.244 (iso). Here the gain is in the candidate set itself (the same clip where v1 saw +0.21 in top-1 recall) and the solver keeps it. Precision is 0.88-1.0 for both.
    • Against production RT-DETR-L (harness machine labels): B1(5ch) - B1(RT-DETR) = -0.059 [-0.092, -0.029] on the test clips with raw knots (YOLO confidences sit well below RT-DETR's, so the knots mark most YOLO candidates as unreliable and the solver's min_conf 0.3 discards them), +0.024 [-0.003, +0.053] with iso (0.782 vs 0.759) at -0.038 precision, and on arsenal_example -0.330 raw / -0.091 [-0.145, -0.040] iso (RT-DETR 0.540, B iso 0.449). The ceiling (any candidate >= 0.1 within 10 px) stays lower for the YOLO arms (test 0.756 vs 0.829; arsenal_example 0.429 vs 0.631): a 5ch YOLO11s still misses balls that RT-DETR-L proposes. So the temporal input narrows but does not close the gap to the production detector; the RT-DETR-L is the stronger base.
    • Exclusions. Test numbers use the harness exclusions exactly (frames within 5 of a v11 train/valid image dropped; 170 GT balls + 2 no_ball frames). arsenal_example is not a harness clip (no machine labels): applying the harness rule, 167 of its 176 labelled frames lie within 5 frames of a v11 valid image, leaving 9 clean frames (a "dev" clip). The harness-clean table has n = 9 and says nothing; the "all labelled frames" table is a deviation, defensible because the YOLO arms were neither trained on this clip (0 train frames) nor selected on val (last.pt); RT-DETR, selected on valid, is if anything flattered there.
    • Caveats: frames in a clip are strongly correlated and the bootstrap resamples frames (the test set is 65% one clip, seg010), so the CIs are optimistic; one model size, two seeds; isotonic maps from train are over-trusting (see above); the solver parameters were tuned on RT-DETR confidences and are used unchanged (B0 tau 0.4, B1 camera-on set), only min_conf and the B0 threshold are moved to the equal-precision confidence in the iso variant.

    Pipeline and checks¶

    • Decoding: ffmpeg CLI -fps_mode passthrough (no cv2 decoding), one sequential pass per clip, 5-frame window. All 10 clips are 25 fps, 1920x1080; frame counts equal the harness' (ffprobe packets = machine-label manifest frames). Frame indices verified three ways: (1) for 37 dataset images of these clips the ffmpeg frame t equals the dataset TIFF BGR(t) exactly (mean abs diff 0.0; t+-1 gives 3-20), so the indices equal the dataset/GT index space; (2) RT-DETR rerun by this pipeline equals the harness' machine labels: the top-1 candidate of every frame with conf >= 0.3 has the same position (within 3 px) and confidence (within 0.05) in 549/549, 609/610, 311/312, 2293/2294, 212/212, 2126/2132, 426/426, 680/680 and 347/348 frames (seg010 ... seg140); (3) the harness' B1 / B0 on its own machine labels are reproduced to the count (129/170 TP, precision 0.985 and 126/170, 0.947).
    • 5ch input built on the fly like the dataset builder: LK + RANSAC alignment of t-2 and t+2 into t (calibration/temporal, person boxes masked: RT-DETR boxes conf >= 0.3 of the same frame), cut (consecutive-frame histogram distance > 0.2), clip edge and alignment failure -> gray(t). Neighbour slots over the 9 test clips + arsenal_example: at most 8 cut/edge slots per clip, 44 alignment failures (seg115: 42).
    • Candidates: ball class, conf >= 0.1, 1280, boxes in the 1920x1080 frame; camera shift / shot cuts for the solver come from the harness' own estimate (player boxes of the RT-DETR rows; for arsenal_example from the rerun's player boxes), identical for every detector.
    • Isotonic maps: PAV on candidate-level correctness (within 10 px of a labelled ball) of the four YOLO runs on the 1,034 v11 train images (not val/test/heldout), one map per run.
    • GPU: 8,851 frames (10 clips) at about 0.2-0.3 s/frame with RT-DETR + 4 YOLO models + 2 LK alignments per frame, plus 4 x 1,034 train images; one flow run (about 40 min); the flow refuses to start with less than 2 GB of free GPU memory.

    Union test: do the 5ch candidates add to RT-DETR-L?¶

    Per frame the candidates of RT-DETR-L (harness knots -> p) and of a 5ch YOLO arm (isotonic map fitted on v11 valid without arsenal_example -> p) are merged: cross-source duplicates within 6 px collapse to one candidate (higher-p position, p = max), optionally with a +0.5 logit bonus when both agree. The merged p is fed to the harness' B0 (tau 0.4) / B1 (camera-on parameters) unchanged, through the inverse of the harness knots (RT-DETR alone is reproduced exactly). Ceiling of a union row = any candidate of either source (conf >= 0.1) within 10 px. 5ch YOLO = arm B of v1, seeds 0 and 1 reported separately.

    Val isotonic maps (n small: B_s0: 44 candidates (40 correct) from 53 images, 4 knots, p in [0.50, 1.00]; B_s1: 43 candidates (37 correct) from 53 images, 5 knots, p in [0.00, 1.00]).

    Reading: the 5ch candidates add to, rather than replace, the production detector, but modestly. The ceiling rises only a little (test +0.012 / +0.006 for seeds 0 / 1, arsenal_example +0.006 / +0.011) because most 5ch candidates duplicate RT-DETR's (about 6.5k agreeing candidates against about 1k YOLO-only on the test clips). B1 recall rises +0.029 / +0.041 on the test clips (frame CI excludes 0; the clip-level CI touches 0 because 7 of the 9 clips have a single GT frame and seg010 carries 65% of the frames) and +0.062 / +0.023 on arsenal_example; precision is -0.034 (seed 0) / +0.001 (seed 1) on test, so seed 0 also adds some wrong candidates. The agreement bonus changes almost nothing. On the 2 harness no_ball frames of the test clips no configuration emits a point (n = 2: uninformative). The val isotonic maps rest on 43-44 candidates (4-5 knots): too small to trust, so the union numbers are indicative; they do show that the gain over RT-DETR alone does not depend on a well-calibrated YOLO map.
