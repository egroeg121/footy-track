## (A) Offline ball trajectory reconstruction from noisy candidates

### Takeaway
The candidate-then-link pattern (generate many low-threshold detections per frame, then choose a path through them using motion plausibility) is the established recipe, from Yu et al. 2006 (trajectory candidates + Kalman verification, ~81% ball location accuracy) through SoccerNet 2023 entries (low conf 0.05 + polynomial-smoothed gating + interpolation). Strong learned/global options exist (TrackNetV3 InpaintNet, TOTNet, WASB, flow/KSP), but almost all published code is for racket sports with a (near-)static camera, and every physics-aware football method found (3D, flight/possession modes) needs per-frame camera calibration. Nothing found handles pan/tilt/zoom without homography beyond image-space smoothing or BoT-SORT-style global motion compensation.

### Cited Findings

**Classic candidate-graph / trajectory-based**
- Yu, Leong, Xu, Tian, "Trajectory-Based Ball Detection and Tracking in Broadcast Soccer Video", IEEE Trans. Multimedia 8(6):1164-1178, 2006: two phases, per-frame candidate generation then linking candidates into trajectories; abstract reports roughly 81% ball location accuracy. A citing paper describes a Kalman-filter-based verification to choose the true trajectory among candidate trajectories (secondary description, not verified against full text). — [dblp](https://dblp.dagstuhl.de/rec/journals/tmm/YuLXT06.html), [NYCU copy](https://ir.lib.nycu.edu.tw/bitstream/11536/30665/1/000253046500011.pdf)
- A related broadcast-soccer graph method treats each short trajectory as a graph node and runs Dijkstra to select the smoothest, longest path; a patent ("Three-level scheme for efficient ball tracking") uses the same trajectory-graph idea with added start/end nodes so a shortest-path search picks the ball path (both undated, described via search summaries). — [USPTO 8184855](https://image-ppubs.uspto.gov/dirsearch-public/print/downloadPdf/8184855)
- Min-cost flow / k-shortest-paths: Berclaz et al., TPAMI 2011 recast detection linking as a constrained convex flow solved by k-shortest disjoint paths (very fast; each detection node used by one trajectory). Ben Shitrit et al. extended to multi-commodity min-cost max-flow with sparse appearance cues. Zhang/Li/Nevatia 2008 min-cost flow is only known to me via citation (not fetched). — [Berclaz TPAMI record](https://infoscience.epfl.ch/handle/20.500.14299/65062), [BMVC 2015 citing paper](https://www.bmva-archive.org.uk/bmvc/2015/papers/paper004/paper004.pdf)
- Maksai, Wang, Fleuret, Fua, "What Players Do with the Ball", CVPR 2016: ball tracking as global objective minimisation, integer program over a ball graph (multi-camera; the 2025 Yandex paper reports 8 cameras, global MIP over 500 frames, ~6 fps). — [paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2016/papers/Maksai_What_Players_Do_CVPR_2016_paper.pdf), [Yandex 2025 table](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.07981v1)

**Practical 2023 baseline (closest to our setup)**
- SoccerNet 2023 Tracking Challenge, 3rd place (MOT4MOT): separate ball and player tracking. Ball = fine-tuned YOLOv8l at confidence 0.05 (many candidates per frame, 1080x1920); take the most confident detection per frame, smooth its centre with a 3rd-order polynomial over a 51-frame window, keep only the detection nearest the smoothed track if within 100 px, then linearly interpolate gaps. Ball detector AP@0.5 0.95 / AP@0.5:0.95 0.71 (boxes not tight). Final combined HOTA 66.27 (DetA 70.32, AssA 62.62) on challenge set. — [arXiv 2308.16651](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.16651) (read via locally extracted PDF text)
- Another 2023 entry (ZTrackers) used YOLOv5 + ByteTrack with a tuned Kalman filter (search-summary only). — [SoccerNet 2023 results](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.06006)
- SoccerNet-Tracking dataset: 200 sequences of 30 s at 25 fps with the ball annotated; authors note ball often <100 px, occlusion/blur. — [arXiv 2204.06918](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2204.06918)

**Physics/mode-switching (flight vs possession), 2025**
- Yandex "Real-time Localization of a Soccer Ball from a Single Camera" (arXiv 2506.07981, June 2025): online recursive filter with fixed-width beam search (top-K hypotheses scored by log-likelihood; latency parameter L controls how many recent frames stay revisable). Four discrete ball states: Jumping, Player Possession, Wait-after-Possession, Out-of-Pitch. Flight follows gravity with bounces, kick sets free-flight velocity; no explicit rolling/friction model described. Each detection becomes a 3D viewing ray (3 cm steps) so per-frame intrinsics+extrinsics (calibration) are required. Results on ~30 min of Russian Premier League with 4-camera triangulated GT: Acc@0.5 m 0.59 (L=50), @1 m 0.66, @4 m 0.71, kick F1 0.74, 53 fps; Maksai MIP offline: 0.56 / 0.64 / 0.73 (4 m), 6 fps; MIP better at 4-8 m and out-of-pitch F1. Dataset proprietary; no code link found. Note ~40% of frames exceed 50 cm error, so "centimetre-level" is an overstatement. — [arXiv 2506.07981](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.07981v1)
- I found no paper using RANSAC/parabola fitting with flight/roll mode switching on broadcast soccer (searched twice). — see Gaps.

**Learned trajectory models over frame stacks (racket sports; code exists)**
- TrackNetV3 (ACM MM 2023): trajectory prediction (heatmap over frame stack) + rectification module "InpaintNet": repair masks derived from the predicted trajectory, a network inpaints masked points. Training: `generate_mask_data.py` then InpaintNet with `--seq_len 16 --epoch 300 --batch_size 32 --mask_ratio 0.3`; inference takes `--inpaintnet_file`. Repo and checkpoints are MIT. Shuttlecock test accuracy 97.51% (TrackNetV2 94.98%, YOLOv7 57.82% in README vs 53.47% in paper - discrepancy). Pretrained weights are badminton (Google Drive zip); data format is simply `Frame,Visibility,X,Y` per frame, so InpaintNet is conceptually trainable on any 2D candidate sequence (this is my inference). — [GitHub qaz812345/TrackNetV3](https://github.com/qaz812345/TrackNetV3), [paper PDF](https://people.cs.nycu.edu.tw/~yushuen/data/TrackNetV3.pdf)
- WASB-SBDT (BMVC 2023, NTT): "widely applicable strong baseline": high-resolution feature extraction, position-aware training, temporal-consistency-aware inference; compares 6 SOTA methods on 5 sports datasets including soccer; MIT; first release was eval code of pretrained models only (current state unverified). — [GitHub nttcom/WASB-SBDT](https://github.com/nttcom/WASB-SBDT), [arXiv 2311.05237](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2311.05237)
- TOTNet (arXiv 2508.09650, Aug 2025): 3D convolutions + visibility-weighted loss + occlusion augmentation (masking ball area in target frame so the model must use temporal/spatial context). Tennis/badminton/table tennis; RMSE 37.30 -> 7.19 and fully-occluded accuracy 0.63 -> 0.80 on table tennis TTA dataset. 5 input frames (more did not help); authors say it works with past frames only. Code MIT; TTA dataset research-only, commercial use prohibited; WASB model used from its own repo; pretrained weights not confirmed. — [GitHub AugustRushG/TOTNet](https://github.com/AugustRushG/TOTNet), [arXiv 2508.09650](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.09650)
- TrackNetV5 (arXiv Dec 2025): tennis heatmap tracker with motion-direction decoupling + residual refinement for occlusion; F1 0.9859 on TrackNetV2 dataset (search-summary only). A 2024 Chalmers thesis found TrackNetV3 works for fast unoccluded balls but struggles when the ball is still or frequently occluded (dribbling) (search-summary only). — [TrackNetV5 arXiv 2512.02789](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.02789), [Chalmers thesis](https://odr.chalmers.se/bitstreams/2d749454-53fd-47ed-b19b-dc8646171bd2/download)

**Ball inference from player trajectories (candidate-free prior / imputation)**
- BallRadar (Kim et al., KDD 2023, arXiv 2306.08206): Set Transformer + hierarchical Bi-LSTM, first predicts player-level possession (intermediate target) then trajectory; "reality loss" + rule-based post-processing so the ball only changes direction when kicked/carried. Mean position error <3.7 m, possession accuracy 64.7% (on GPS+event data from 15 matches, internal; plus 3 public Metrica matches). Inputs are pitch-coordinate player tracks. Code at github.com/pientist/ballradar: no licence visible, no pretrained weights, trained on Metrica sample data by script. — [arXiv 2306.08206](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.08206), [GitHub pientist/ballradar](https://github.com/pientist/ballradar)
- Peral et al., "Multi-Modal Soccer Scene Analysis with Masked Pre-Training" (arXiv 2512.19528, Dec 2025): multi-task transformer (ball trajectory, ball state, possessor) from player pitch-coordinate trajectories + player type + per-player image crops from a tactical camera; ball NOT an input. Proprietary data: 283 matches, 43,397/3,968/3,347 train/val/test sequences of 60 frames at 6.25 Hz. Ball ADE 1.17 m (MaxErr 4.77) vs TranSPORTmer 1.72 m and BallRadar 1.86 m; possessor acc 89.56% (vs 83.79 / 78.12); ball-state acc 85.85%. CropDrop pretraining masks visual features. No code found. — [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.19528), [HTML v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.19528v1)
- Diffusion-style completion: U2Diff (CVPR 2025) does trajectory completion/imputation/forecasting on NBA, Basketball-U, Football-U, Soccer-U; its TPAMI extension is arXiv 2605.10717. JointDiff (arXiv 2509.22522) jointly generates player+ball trajectories and possession events (NBA, NFL, Bundesliga) with weak-possessor and text guidance; project page guillem-cf.github.io/JointDiff. I did not confirm any of them evaluates ball-only imputation, nor code/licence. — [U2Diff](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.18589v1), [JointDiff](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22522), [2605.10717](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.10717)

**Camera motion without homography**
- None of the above football methods (BallRadar, Peral, Yandex, SportsSUSHI) operate without calibration/pitch coordinates. MOT4MOT's ball pipeline works purely in image space with a 100 px gate and 51-frame polynomial, i.e. it tolerates pans only because the ball moves faster than the smoothing window drifts (my inference). The project's own KB page already flags BoT-SORT's camera-motion compensation as the available image-space fix. — [KB: footy-ball-detection-findings](file:///home/george/knowledge/wiki/footy-ball-detection-findings.md)

### Inferences
- A cheap, buildable global solver for us: per-clip graph whose nodes are all detections at conf >= ~0.3-0.5 (plus a "missing" state), edge costs from camera-compensated image-space motion + detector confidence (calibrated tiers in our data: >=0.96 precision above 0.7), solved by Viterbi/DP or min-cost-flow/KSP with a single path (one ball). This is the Yu/MOT4MOT idea made globally optimal both directions; no off-the-shelf repo found for football - must build (small).
- Flight vs roll mode switching is best done after a homography exists (3D flight is a parabola in pitch coordinates, not in image space under zoom/pan). Before homography, restrict to local polynomial/Kalman-RTS in camera-compensated image space with high process noise at contact events.
- TrackNetV3's InpaintNet (MIT, takes sequences of coordinates+mask) is the most reusable learned gap-filler: it can be retrained on our human-checked ball tracks with synthetic masks (mask_ratio 0.3), but camera pans confuse image-space priors unless coordinates are camera-compensated (inference, untested).
- Player-conditioned ball inference (BallRadar/Peral) needs pitch coordinates and, for Peral, a much larger dataset; errors of 1-4 m are far coarser than detector-supported localisation, so the useful role is a prior/possession gate, not the trajectory itself.

### Gaps
- No source found for RANSAC/parabola fitting with flight/roll mode switching on broadcast soccer; no source for particle smoothers specific to ball tracking; Kalman/RTS bidirectional smoothing is standard but I have no ball-specific cited source.
- No 2025-2026 transformer-over-ball-candidates paper found for football (searched extended); TOTNet/TrackNetV5/WASB are heatmap-over-frames, not candidate-set models.
- Could not read full Yu et al. text or Zhang 2008; licences for BallRadar, U2Diff, JointDiff not confirmed; TOTNet and WASB pretrained weight availability not confirmed; whether WASB soccer weights exist is unverified.
- Whether any method handles broadcast pan/tilt/zoom without homography for the ball: not found.

## (B) Offline / long-horizon player MOT

### Takeaway
Fragmented tracklets (our median 13-31 frames) are the textbook case for an offline hierarchical-graph or tracklet-stitching post-processor. Best-evidenced options: GTA-Link (MIT, model-agnostic, drop-in, SportsMOT/SoccerNet) for merge/split of an existing tracker's output, and SportsSUSHI/SUSHI (hierarchical GNN, truly offline; SUSHI code MIT) for the heavier route, but SportsSUSHI's large gains rely on a per-frame homography, jersey numbers and re-id features we lack. CAMELTrack (Apache-2.0, weights provided) is a strong learned online associator, not offline. The end-to-end MOTR-family trackers are online/propagation-based and trained on dancers/SportsMOT, not long-horizon re-id.

### Cited Findings

**Offline / global**
- SUSHI (Cetintas, Brasó, Leal-Taixé, CVPR 2023): offline, recursively builds a hierarchy of subclip graphs, GNN classifies edges at each level, linear program yields trajectories. Repo dvl-tum/SUSHI is MIT; needs fast-reid; pretrained models/detections via Google Drive. Reported: MOT17 private HOTA 66.5 / IDF1 83.1; MOT20 private HOTA 64.3; DanceTrack test HOTA 63.3 (AssA 50.1, DetA 80.1); BDD IDF1 76.2. — [GitHub](https://github.com/dvl-tum/SUSHI), [arXiv 2212.03038](https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.03038)
- SportsSUSHI ("Towards long-term player tracking with graph hierarchies and domain-specific features", arXiv 2502.21242, Feb 2025): offline; each node keeps 10 nearest candidates; long clips via sliding window + stitching. Features: jersey-number vector (PARSeq, 100-way), one-hot team (A/B/referee), field coordinates via per-frame homography (field registration of Gutiérrez-Pérez et al.; camera parameters linearly interpolated where registration fails), FastReID ResNet50-IBN fine-tuned. SoccerNet tracking (57 train/49 test clips, avg 750 frames): YOLOX detections HOTA 71.36 (AssA 69.99, DetA 72.87) vs ByteTrack 60.56; with GT detections HOTA 90.92 vs SUSHI 85.79. Ablation: field position raised HOTA 85.37 -> 89.22, jersey numbers -> 89.78 (9 layers, GT dets). IDF1 and SportsMOT not reported. Code in repo mkoshkina/sports-SUSHI (licence not checked; weights not explicitly stated as released). — [arXiv HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.21242v1), [repo](https://github.com/mkoshkina/sports-SUSHI)
- GTA: Global Tracklet Association (Sun et al., arXiv 2411.08216, ACCV 2024 workshop): model-agnostic offline post-processor. Tracklet Splitter (DBSCAN on embeddings to find ID switches inside a tracklet) + Tracklet Connector (merge tracklets by averaged cosine distance of embeddings, with spatio-temporal constraints). No training needed but clustering params need tuning (defaults: min_len 100, eps 0.6, min_samples 10, max_k 3, merge_dist_thres 0.4). Deep-EIoU+GTA: SportsMOT HOTA 77.21 -> 81.04 (IDsw 2909 -> 2737); SoccerNet HOTA 79.41 -> 83.11 (IDsw 2803 -> 2188). Repo sjc042/gta-link is MIT; ships a sports ReID checkpoint (`sports_model.pth.tar-60`) via torchreid. — [GitHub](https://github.com/sjc042/gta-link), [arXiv 2411.08216](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.08216)
- GTATrack (arXiv 2602.00484, Jan 2026): SoccerTrack Challenge 2025 winner (static fisheye cameras): Deep-EIoU online association + global tracklet association + pseudo-label detector improvement for small/distorted targets; HOTA 0.60, FP reduced to 982. Code github.com/ron941/GTATrack-STC2025 (licence not checked). — [arXiv 2602.00484](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.00484)
- SoccerNet GSR 2025 winner (KIST, GS-HOTA 63.90): YOLOX + Deep-EIoU with OSNet ReID, tracklets refined by split-and-merge (splits from identity-prediction consistency, merges from ReID similarity under spatio-temporal and identity constraints), LLaMA-3.2-Vision predicting role/jersey/colour, majority voting within tracklets, team affiliation by clustering jersey colour and mean x-position. — [SoccerNet 2025 results, arXiv 2508.19182](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.19182) (read via local extracted text)

**Learned association / end-to-end**
- CAMELTrack (Standaert et al., arXiv 2505.01257, May 2025; UCLouvain/EPFL/Sportradar): learned association (temporal encoders per cue + group-aware fusion transformer GAFFE) on top of any detector; online, built on TrackLab; code Apache-2.0 at TrackingLaboratory/CAMELTrack; HF weights for DanceTrack (HOTA 66.1, 69.3 with keypoints), SportsMOT (80.3 with appearance+keypoints; paper says 80.4), MOT17 62.4, PoseTrack21 66.0, BEE24 50.3; default checkpoint is joint MOT17/DanceTrack/SportsMOT/PoseTrack21. README does not list SoccerNet; custom MOT-format datasets supported. An aggregator lists "SoccerNet (test) HOTA 54.2" - unverified which benchmark. Its tracklet-level temporal encoders mean it could be run with longer memory, but it is described as online. — [GitHub](https://github.com/TrackingLaboratory/CAMELTrack), [arXiv 2505.01257](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.01257)
- MOTIP (Gao & Wang, CVPR 2025, arXiv 2403.16848): MOT as ID prediction with Deformable DETR (R50); SportsMOT HOTA 71.9 (75.2 if trained on train+val); repo MCG-NJU/MOTIP Apache-2.0; checkpoints on cloud/Drive (MODEL_ZOO); appears frame-by-frame (inference; not stated). — [GitHub](https://github.com/MCG-NJU/MOTIP), [HyperAI SportsMOT board](https://next.hyper.ai/en/sota/tasks/multiple-object-tracking/benchmark/multiple-object-tracking-on-sportsmot)
- SambaMOTR (Segu et al., ICLR 2025): tracking-by-propagation with synchronised long-term memory (Samba); trained with gradients on last 5 frames of long sequences. SportsMOT HOTA 70.5 (Def-DETR) / 72.1 (DAB-D-DETR); DanceTrack 67.2 / 69.0. MIT; HF weights at `mattiasegu/sambamotr`; Python 3.11, PyTorch 2.5.1, compiled deformable-attention ops. Online/autoregressive (inferred, not stated). — [GitHub](https://github.com/mattiasegu/sambamotr), [arXiv 2410.01806](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.01806v1)
- MeMOTR (long-term memory MOTR): DanceTrack HOTA 68.5 (AssA 58.4), repo mcg-nju/memotr. — [GitHub](https://github.com/mcg-nju/memotr)
- MOTRv3 and MPNTrack/ReMOTS: not retrieved (see Gaps).
- SportsMOT leaderboard snapshots (mixed splits, partly self-reported): HAT-FastReID-MOT 80.8 (Mar 2025), CAMELTrack 80.4, Deep HM-SORT 80.1 (extra data), DeepEIoU+GTA 81.0, MeMoSORT 82.1 (self-reported, arXiv 2508.09796, unverified), DiffMOT 76.2, McByte 76.9, MixSort-OC 74.1, OC-SORT 73.7. SportsMOT itself excludes the ball (per project KB). — [paperswithcode2](https://paperswithcode2.com/benchmark/50188), [CVPRW 2025 supp.](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025W/CVSPORTS/supplemental/Stanczyk_No_Train_Yet_CVPRW_2025_supplemental.pdf), [MeMoSORT](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.09796v1)

**Re-id / jersey**
- PRTreID (MMSports 2023; Somers et al., arXiv 2401.09942, CC BY-SA 4.0 paper): multi-task re-id + team affiliation + role classification built on BPBreID; evaluated on SoccerNet-GSR/Tracking; repo github.com/VlSomers/Prtreid can auto-extract a re-id dataset from SoccerNet tracking for training; at the time of search results, weight-download instructions were "to come" (current status unverified). BPBreID weights are for generic person re-id (Market, Duke, Occluded-*). sn-gamestate's released baseline states are labelled "prtreid-strongsort". — [PRTreID repo](https://github.com/VlSomers/Prtreid), [arXiv 2401.09942](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.09942), [BPBreID](https://github.com/VlSomers/bpbreid)
- MOT4MOT fine-tuned OSNet-ain (x0.25 to x1, crops 256x128) on SoccerNet IDs using jersey number metadata to keep IDs consistent across clips, only 199 IDs / 6 games; appearance post-processing merging in their pipeline. — [arXiv 2308.16651](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.16651)
- Jersey number: SoccerNet Jersey Number dataset 2853 tracklets (hidden challenge set 1211); 2023 leader ZZPM 92.85% accuracy. 2025 GSR entries used PARSeq on torso crops (4th place 61.64 GS-HOTA) and VLMs (LLaMA-Vision, Qwen2-VL); CLIP- and OSNet-based features were popular. — [sn-jersey](https://github.com/SoccerNet/sn-jersey), [SoccerNet 2025 results](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.19182)
- TeamTrack (arXiv 2404.13868): multi-sport full-pitch MOT dataset (not read). — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.13868)

### Inferences
- Lowest effort / highest evidence-of-fit: keep our detector, run a better online tracker (Deep-EIoU, or CAMELTrack with its pretrained SportsMOT weights) and then GTA-Link as offline merge/split. GTA gave +3.7 HOTA on SoccerNet (79.4 -> 83.1) at tuning cost only.
- GTA-Link and SUSHI both depend on embeddings; our ImageNet ResNet50 re-id is at chance across clips, so the GTA connector would merge poorly across clips; within a single clip (one continuous camera) re-id needs only to separate ~22 people over minutes, which is much easier than cross-clip. Fine-tuning a sports re-id (PRTreID or OSNet on SoccerNet crops, or GTA's shipped sports checkpoint) is a prerequisite.
- SportsSUSHI shows the single biggest domain feature is pitch position (+3.9 HOTA on GT dets); that is blocked until the planned homography exists, which strengthens the case to prioritise calibration (pnlcalib/NBJW via sn-gamestate) for MOT as well as ball.
- SUSHI/SportsSUSHI is the only genuinely whole-clip bidirectional learned method found with open code (SUSHI), but SportsSUSHI's trained models were not confirmed released; building it means training on SoccerNet tracking with our features.
- Online MOTR-family models (MOTIP, SambaMOTR) trained on DanceTrack/SportsMOT underperform 80+ HOTA association-based systems on SportsMOT (71.9, 72.1 vs 80.3-81.0) and are not offline; not a priority.

### Gaps
- Licence of Deep-EIoU, TrackLab, SportsSUSHI, GTATrack repos not checked; Ultralytics/YOLOX licences (AGPL/Apache) are background knowledge, not verified here. SoccerNet/SportsMOT dataset licences (SoccerNet data typically under NDA; non-commercial) not verified - flag as likely non-commercial.
- MOTRv2/MOTRv3, MPNTrack, ReMOTS: not retrieved. SoccerNet-Tracking HOTA tables for current SOTA not retrieved. TeamTrack results not read.
- Whether SportsSUSHI weights are public: unconfirmed.
- GTA-Link exact per-dataset parameters for football broadcast with pan/zoom: not tested.

## (C) Joint ball + player reasoning

### Takeaway
Joint models exist for the player->ball direction (BallRadar, Peral et al., diffusion trajectory models) but all take pitch-coordinate player tracks and none has public code with weights. SoccerNet GSR / sn-gamestate does not reconstruct the ball at all (task outputs are athletes, roles, jersey, team, pitch position). The most practical joint use for us is therefore a possession/proximity constraint inside our own ball solver, not an adopted joint model.

### Cited Findings
- sn-gamestate (SoccerNet/sn-gamestate, GPL-3.0, built on TrackLab): modules YOLOv11 detection, PRTReid re-id, TVCalib by default (PnLCalib or "No Bells, Just Whistles" selectable), MMOCR jersey number, team affiliation; baseline tracker states "prtreid-strongsort"; models auto-download on first run; the README does not mention the ball, and the GSR task description lists athlete positions/roles/team/jersey. Baseline GS-HOTA 29.01 (2025 leaderboard); winner 63.90. — [GitHub](https://github.com/SoccerNet/sn-gamestate), [SoccerNet 2025 results](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.19182), [GSR paper arXiv 2404.11335](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.11335v1)
- GSR 2025 calibration trends: NBJW, PnLCalib, Broadtrack; optical flow added for temporal smoothing of calibration (KIST winner). — [SoccerNet 2025 results](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.19182)
- Possession/state as an intermediate variable: BallRadar predicts possessor first (64.7% acc) then trajectory with direction-change-only-at-contact post-processing; Peral et al. predict possessor 89.56% and ball state 85.85%; Yandex uses explicit Player-Possession/Wait-after-Possession modes in its beam search. — [BallRadar](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.08206), [Peral](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.19528v1), [Yandex](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.07981v1)
- Peral et al. report that image crops of players carry ball-action cues (visual branch pre-trained on a ball-related-action classification task) and that CropDrop pretraining is needed to avoid over-reliance. — [HTML v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.19528v1)
- JointDiff jointly generates all agents + ball + possession events; conditioning on a possessor list (weak-possessor guidance) is its control mechanism. — [arXiv 2509.22522](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22522)
- Related ice hockey puck localisation using contextual cues (arXiv 2506.04365) appeared in search; not read. — [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.04365)

### Inferences
- A joint solver we can build without those models: add to the ball path cost a term that favours the ball being near some player's feet/at their image position during "possession" segments (derived from player tracks), and allows free-flight segments between possessions (Yandex-style 2-4 mode HMM). Needs only image-space player boxes (feet = bbox bottom-centre) and works without homography as a soft prior; mode-timing logic would use image-space motion until calibration lands.
- Pretrained transformers trained on 6.25 Hz pitch-coordinate tracks (BallRadar, Peral) are not directly transferable to 25 fps broadcast with moving cameras; treat them as design references (possessor token, set attention over players, reality loss) rather than weights to fine-tune.
- GSR pipelines are useful only for the player/calibration half; the ball must be our own module.

### Gaps
- No public football work found that jointly refines both ball and player tracks from broadcast video (as opposed to ball-from-players). No ball-in-GSR baseline found. Peral/BallRadar/JointDiff generalisation to broadcast-derived noisy tracks unreported in what I read.
