# Offline ball-tracking baseline (B0 / B1 / B2 / B3)

Generated by `scripts/eval_offline_ball.py` (code: `src/footy_track/ball_offline/`). All distances in pixels at 1920x1080. Detector: RT-DETR-L @1280, conf>=0.1 (`machine_labels/`).

## Findings (hand-written summary of this run; the tables below are generated)

- Test set: 9 clips, **170 clean ball instances** (110 of them seg010, 53 seg031, 7 single-frame clips), 2 explicit `no_ball` frames. Intervals are wide; per-clip spread is only meaningful for seg010 and seg031.
- B1 (offline Viterbi + short gap fill, camera-stabilised) vs B0 (top-1 per frame, tau chosen on dev): recall@10 0.759 vs 0.741 (+3 frames of 170, not significant), precision 0.985 vs 0.947 (2 vs 7 wrong points). The gain is mostly *removing wrong top-1 picks*, not recovering misses.
- The ceiling (any candidate >=0.1 within 10 px) is 141/170 = 0.829; B1 recovers 129. The 29 un-hittable frames are detector misses (23 mid-clip, 4 clip start, 2 fast pan). The 12 reachable-but-missed frames hold candidates with conf 0.10-0.30; those bands are ~5% precise on GT frames (16/342 at 0.1-0.2), so neither the DP nor B2 rescue admits them. B2 equals B1 here.
- B3 (human anchors every 10/25/50th labelled frame) adds nothing: anchors are 53-239 frames apart (labels are clustered), beyond the 25-frame link/fill limit, and anchors-only interpolation is useless (recall <= 0.014 at 10 px).
- Candidate-level precision on GT frames (test, conf band: correct/candidates): 0.1-0.2: 16/342, 0.2-0.3: 5/84, 0.3-0.4: 4/40, 0.4-0.6: 10/29, 0.6-0.7: 10/10, >=0.7: 113/113. The 0.4-0.6 band is less precise than the interpolated calibration knots assume (0.5-0.72).
- Independent Ball Check cross-check (section 8): confirmed-ball recall 0.929 (B1) vs 0.902 (B0); confirmed-not-ball emission 0.164 vs 0.243.

## 7. Caveats

- GT is sparse (a few percent of frames are labelled), so precision is measured only on labelled frames and a prediction on an unlabelled frame is never penalised; absolute precision is therefore optimistic for every method, including B1's interpolated points.
- Dev clips are detector-contaminated, so confidence calibration/thresholds tuned there are likely too trusting of confidence. Test numbers are the honest ones; test is small, hence the Wilson intervals.
- No homography; camera motion is a global translation estimated from player-box centres (see camera diagnostics in the JSON). Shot cuts are flagged heuristically (low support of the dominant player displacement) and unverified against the video.
- Calibration knots between the measured Ball Check bands are interpolated assumptions.
