Read this before trusting the output.
  • The ball is found in only about 6 of every 10 frames where it is visible (recall about 0.6). The ball track can also lock onto a false positive, for example a player's head, and follow it for a long time.
  • Players and referees are tracked frame to frame only: no team colours and no re-identification, so the same person gets a new id after being lost.
  • Tracks restart at every broadcast cut, so ids are only meaningful inside one clip.
